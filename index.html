Zone 1 · Gas

Explosive gas atmospheres likely in normal operation

Designed for areas where flammable gas or vapour mixtures are expected to occur periodically during routine work.

Zone 21 · Dust

Combustible dust clouds likely in normal operation

Designed for environments where combustible dust is expected to be present occasionally, such as milling, processing and bulk handling.

Ex ia IIC T4

Intrinsically safe · highest gas group · T4 temperature class
